The hands behind the beauty

The art of the stitch

Hand embroidery asks for patience, precision and imagination. We believe its beauty lives in every thoughtful detail.

A living tradition

Beauty made slowly, by skilled hands.

Our design language is inspired by the richness of Indian textile traditions and the artistry of hand embellishment. We value the human touch: the measured rhythm of a needle, the balance of a motif and the character that only handwork can bring.

We work with embroidery craftsmanship connected to Lucknow and the wider artisan traditions of Uttar Pradesh, while keeping each design personal and contemporary.
